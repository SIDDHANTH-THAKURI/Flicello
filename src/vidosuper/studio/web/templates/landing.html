05You

And it learns you.

Rate a film and say why. Flicello turns your words into rules for the next one: your pace, your colours, how much text you like. Each film fits you a little better than the last.

  • “less text”→one title, no captions
  • “faster after the drop”→half-beat shots after the drop
  • “warmer”→golden grade by default
