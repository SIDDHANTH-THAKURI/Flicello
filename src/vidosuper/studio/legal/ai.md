## What the AI does

Flicello uses AI the way an editor's assistant would:

- **Watching your clips.** It reads still frames, motion and sound measurements, and transcripts, then describes what
  happens and when.
- **Finding stories.** It pitches different films hidden in the same footage, each with a beginning, a turn and an
  ending.
- **Planning the edit.** It chooses shots, their order and their timing on the beat, the colour look, titles and,
  for talking videos, motion graphics placed on your words.
- **Learning your style.** It turns your ratings and comments into rules for your next videos. You can see and edit
  those rules on your style page.

The planning is done by Anthropic's Claude models, through Anthropic's commercial API.

## What runs on our own servers

Speech-to-text, beat and music analysis, separating a person from the background, colour grading, and rendering the
final video all run on our servers, not at an AI provider.

## What the AI doesn't do

- It isn't trained on your clips or your films, and neither are our providers' models.
- It doesn't recognise or identify people, and it doesn't infer emotions or sensitive traits.
- It doesn't make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## You stay in control

You choose the story, review a preview, ask for changes in plain words, and decide whether to publish. Nothing is
posted anywhere automatically.

## Limits worth knowing

AI can misjudge a moment, mishear speech, pick a weaker shot or misplace a graphic. Watch your video before you
publish it, and tell Flicello what to fix.

## Labelling AI content

Flicello edits footage that you filmed. Some elements can be AI-made: for example, a music bed composed for you. Where
we add AI-generated material, we say so in the app.

If you publish realistic content that was generated or substantially altered by AI, platform rules and some laws
(such as the EU AI Act, and India's rules on synthetically generated information) may require you to label it. We don't
generate realistic depictions of real people without their consent.
