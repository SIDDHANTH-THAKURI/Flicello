## Who processes data for us

These providers process personal information only to provide their service to us, under contracts that require them
to protect it.

| Provider | What they do | What they receive | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| DigitalOcean, LLC | Servers and storage | All account data and files | {{ hosting }} |
| Anthropic, PBC | AI analysis and edit planning | Still frames, transcripts and your written requests | United States |
| Namecheap, Inc. | Domain name and DNS | No account data | United States |
| Internet Security Research Group (Let's Encrypt) | HTTPS certificates | No account data | United States |

## Content libraries

To find animations and library music that fit your video, we send short search words (such as "ocean" or "popcorn")
to LottieFiles, Openverse and Jamendo. We never send them your footage or any personal information.
