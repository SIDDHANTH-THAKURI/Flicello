title: Copyright and takedowns
summary: If you believe something on Flicello infringes your copyright, tell us and we'll act quickly. If your content was removed by mistake, you can ask us to put it back.
updated: 17 October 2026

## Respecting copyright

Flicello users must only upload material they have the right to use. Projects on Flicello are private to the person who
made them, but we still act on infringement reports about anything we store.

## Reporting infringement

Email **{{ email.copyright }}** with:

1. the work you believe is being infringed (for example, a song title and its artist, or a link to your original);
2. where it is on Flicello: a link, or enough detail for us to find it;
3. your name, address, phone number and email;
4. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use isn't authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you're the owner of the right, or authorised
   to act for the owner;
6. your physical or electronic signature.

These are the elements of a notice under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act. We accept notices from any country.

## What happens next

When we receive a complete notice, we remove or disable access to the material promptly and tell the person who
uploaded it. We may share your notice, without your contact details, with that person.

## If your content was removed by mistake

If you believe material was removed because of a mistake or misidentification, send a counter-notice to
**{{ email.copyright }}** with:

1. what was removed, and where it was;
2. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or
   misidentification;
3. your name, address and phone number;
4. a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the courts where you live (or, if you live outside the US, any
   judicial district where Flicello could be found), and that you'll accept service of process from the person who sent
   the notice;
5. your physical or electronic signature.

We'll pass the counter-notice to the person who complained. If they don't tell us within 10 to 14 business days that
they've started court action, we may restore the material.

## Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of people who repeatedly infringe copyright.

## Please don't abuse this process

Knowingly false notices or counter-notices can have legal consequences.
