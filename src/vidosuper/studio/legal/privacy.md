## What we collect

**Account information.** What you'd like to be called, your email address and, if you use "Sign in with Google", your
Google account ID and name. If you add a passkey, we store only its public key: your fingerprint or face never leaves
your device. We also keep your confirmation that you're {{ min_age }} or over, which version of the Terms and of this
policy you accepted and when, together with a keyed, one-way hash of your IP address, as a record of that consent.

**Your content.** The clips, photos, songs and voice recordings you upload; what you ask for (titles, notes, requests);
the stories we pitch to you and the videos we make; your ratings and comments; and your style profile (the rules we
learn from your ratings, which you can see and edit on your style page).

**Usage and device information.** Sign-in records (when, from which browser, and your IP address), used to keep your
account safe. Which pages and features are used, counted without cookies (see the Cookie notice). Your approximate
country, worked out from your IP address and not stored with it. Technical logs (errors and timings), which can include
IP addresses.

**What we don't collect.** We don't recognise or identify the people in your footage. When we separate a person from
the background (for talking videos), that happens on our own computers and isn't used to identify anyone. We don't
collect payment details: Flicello is free during early access. We don't knowingly collect information about anyone
under {{ min_age }}.

## Sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares three things with us: your email address (with Google's
confirmation that it's verified), your name, and an ID for your Google account. We ask only for these basic sign-in
details (the `openid`, `email` and `profile` permissions). Google may also send your profile photo, which we don't
keep. We never see your Google password, and we can't access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or anything else in
your Google account.

We use these details only to create your account, sign you in, suggest the name we call you (you can change it) and
send you the emails described below. We keep them with your account and delete them when you delete it. We don't sell
them, share them for advertising or use them to train AI models, and only the service providers that run Flicello for
us ever handle them. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Flicello's access at any time in your Google Account, under Security, "Your connections to third-party
apps & services". That stops Google sign-in but doesn't delete your Flicello account, which you can do from your
account page.

## AI and automated processing

To edit your video, we send still frames from your clips, short transcripts of what's said, and your written requests
to our AI provider, Anthropic (the maker of Claude). Anthropic analyses them and plans the edit for us. It processes
this data on our behalf under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models, and keeps it only for a
limited time. Speech-to-text, beat detection, person cut-outs and rendering run on our own computers.

Two more providers make parts of some videos, from text only. To paint the look you pick for a voice or talking video,
we send text descriptions of its scenes (written by our AI from your request and what is said) to Google's Gemini
image model. To make music for your film, when it uses music made for it, we send a short description of the sound
(written by our AI from your words about the music and your film) to ElevenLabs. Neither receives your footage or your
voice.

The AI makes creative choices: which shots, in what order, which story. No decision with legal or similarly
significant effects on you is made by automated means. You always choose the story, can ask for changes in plain
words, and decide what you publish. More detail: [AI transparency](/legal/ai).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU and EEA, the UK, Australia, India, California and other US states), you
can:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy. Download it from your account page.
- **correct** it if it's wrong.
- **delete** it: delete single projects at any time, or your whole account from the account page.
- **take it with you**: the download is a machine-readable file.
- **object** to some processing, or ask us to **restrict** it.
- **withdraw consent**, for example to product emails, at any time.
- **nominate** someone to act for you if you die or can't act yourself (India).
- **not be treated worse** for using any of these rights.

Use your account page, or email **{{ email.privacy }}**. We reply within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires, and
may need to confirm it's you first.

You can also complain to a regulator: the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), your data
protection authority in the EU or EEA, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or the Data Protection
Board of India. We'd be grateful for the chance to put things right first.

instead of passwords, and your files can only be reached through your own account. No system is perfectly secure. If a
breach is likely to harm you, we'll tell you and the relevant regulators as the law requires. More detail:
[Security](/legal/security).
