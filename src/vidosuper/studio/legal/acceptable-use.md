title: Acceptable Use Policy
summary: Make anything creative and lawful. Don't use Flicello to hurt, deceive or exploit anyone, or to break the Service.
updated: 17 October 2026

## The idea

Flicello exists to turn your own footage into films. This policy sets out what isn't allowed, so the Service stays safe
for everyone. It's part of our [Terms](/legal/terms).

## Don't upload or make

- **Anything illegal** where you live, or where the people in it live.
- **Child sexual abuse material.** We have zero tolerance: we remove it, close the account and report it to the
  authorities.
- **Sexually explicit content.** Flicello isn't for pornography, including AI-altered or "nudified" images of anyone.
- **Real people shown in ways they haven't agreed to.** That includes deepfakes, impersonation, intimate images, and
  content made to humiliate or expose someone.
- **Harassment, bullying, threats,** or content that attacks people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion,
  caste, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age.
- **Content that promotes violence,** terrorism, self-harm or dangerous acts.
- **Deception:** fake news, election or health misinformation, fraudulent ads, scams, or videos presented as real
  events when they aren't.
- **Content that infringes** someone else's copyright, trademark, privacy or publicity rights.
- **Other people's personal information** (documents, addresses, private messages) shared without a lawful reason.

## Don't misuse the Service

- Don't make extra accounts to get around limits, or share accounts.
- Don't try to break, probe, overload or get round our security or limits. If you've found a weakness, please report it:
  see [Security](/legal/security).
- Don't scrape Flicello, or access it by automated means, except through features we provide for that.
- Don't upload malware or anything else designed to cause harm.
- Don't use Flicello, or anything it produces, to build a competing dataset or AI model.

## Music and footage that isn't yours

Only upload songs and footage you have the right to use where you plan to publish. If a platform mutes or removes your
video because of music rights, that's between you and the rights holder.

## Reporting

See something that breaks this policy? Email **{{ email.abuse }}** with a description, and a link or project details

## What we do

Depending on how serious and how repeated the problem is, we may remove content, warn you, limit features, suspend or
close your account, and report illegal content to the authorities where the law requires it. Where it's lawful and
safe, we tell you what we did and why. You can ask us to review a decision by replying to our message or by writing to
{{ email.abuse }}.
