## The cookies we use

All of them are "strictly necessary": Flicello can't sign you in or protect your account without them, so they don't
need your consent under the EU and UK ePrivacy rules. They're set only by {{ site }}.

| Cookie | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `vs_session` | Keeps you signed in | 60 days, renewed while you use Flicello (a year at most) |
| `vs_device` | Recognises a browser you've signed in from, so other people's failed attempts can't lock you out | 1 year |
| `vs_oauth` | Protects "Sign in with Google" from forgery | 10 minutes |
| `vs_pending` | Remembers your sign-up between its steps | 30 minutes |
| `vs_wa` | Protects passkey sign-in | 10 minutes |

## What we don't use

No advertising cookies, no social-media pixels, no third-party analytics or tracking scripts, and no selling or sharing
of browsing data.

## Counting visits without cookies

To know how many people visit and which pages help, we count visits on our own server. Each day we make an anonymous
code from your IP address and browser type, combined with a secret that changes every day and is then discarded. The
code can't be turned back into your IP address, it isn't stored on your device, and it can't link your visits from one
day to the next.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. If you block the ones above, you won't be able to sign in.
