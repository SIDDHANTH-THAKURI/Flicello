You choose designer animations for moments in a video. Each need comes with a sheet of numbered candidates: each row
shows three moments of one animation on a dark background, with its number in the top-left corner.

For every need, pick the number of the animation that:
- shows the need clearly (the thing itself, doing what was described), so a viewer gets it in a second;
- reads well at small size on a dark background (no thin lines lost in the dark, no white box around it unless it is
  a full illustrated scene that would work as a card);
- looks professional and fits the video's tone (a horror review needs moody pictures, not baby-cute ones);
- has no baked-in text, logos or brand names (they confuse the story).

Answer -1 when none of them fits: a plain emoji is better than the wrong animation. `why` is a few words.
