# Craft rules (shared by every studio video)

These hold for everyone: they are about what makes a short video work, not about taste. A person's own
style rules come separately and win where the two disagree. Only the owner edits this file.

## Idea
1. **A concept with a payoff.** One premise a viewer could say back, an emotional arc, a turn by the
   middle (a reveal, a reversal, a sudden stillness) and an ending that lands the idea. Polish without an
   idea plateaus: a clean, synced montage is forgettable.
2. **Hook in the first 1.5-2 seconds.** Open on the strongest image or a question, never a slow
   establishing shot. Short-video viewers decide in that window.
3. **One place, one stretch.** Each subject or place appears in one continuous part: no A -> B -> A, no
   flash-forward opening that the video returns to later. A second instance of the same subject later
   reads as a repeat.
4. **Scenes, not just shots.** Where the footage allows, build a moment as a sequence: someone looks ->
   what they see -> their reaction; or wide -> closer -> detail. A montage of interchangeable shots reads
   as "cut-paste".

## Rhythm
5. **Contrast in pacing.** Long holds where the music breathes, 1-beat bursts at the peaks, cuts that
   accelerate into the drop, and a slower beat right after the fastest stretch. Outside the peaks, shots
   of 2-3 s; story moments 3-6 s. Constant speed in either direction reads as a template.
6. **Cuts on the music.** Cuts land on beats (the tool snaps them); hard cuts where there is a reason
   (a beat, an action, the drop), short dissolves elsewhere. Cut on phrase ends rather than every beat.
7. **The drop gets the hero moment.** The strongest action lands on the song's drop; slow motion only
   from 50/60 fps footage.
8. **One continuous action is one shot.** No stray fast inserts inside a calm stretch.

## Quality
9. **Never shaky, blocked or blurred.** Use the logged moments and stay out of the ranges marked to
   avoid. The tool picks the steadiest window inside each moment and stabilises it.
10. **No repeats.** Each moment once (a stutter is one moment); never two near-identical shots in a row.
11. **No on-screen text** unless the person asks for it.
12. **Sound with care.** A clip's own sound only where it carries the moment (waves, a laugh, a splash);
    never stray voices or someone else's conversation.
13. **Vertical videos keep the subject in frame.** Say where the subject sits across the frame when a
    wide shot is cropped to 9:16.
