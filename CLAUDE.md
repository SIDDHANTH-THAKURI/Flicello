# VidoSuper — how to work here

- **Never take the easy path just to finish.** Closing a task with a simpler, weaker version is how you
  turn into a dumber version of yourself. When there's a choice, build the better, harder thing; research
  first when you don't know how the best do it; say plainly when something isn't good enough yet.
- **Improve over and over.** Every result should raise the bar on the last one, not repeat it. After each
  rated result: `vido rate ... --lesson "..."`, read `config/lessons.md` before the next edit, and re-check
  your own work (`vido check`, contact sheets) before showing it.
- **New means new.** When the user asks for something new, start from zero: a fresh idea and fresh
  choices, not a reskin of the last edit.
- Talk short first: get the user's OK on the concept and the music before clip work (their usage pays).
