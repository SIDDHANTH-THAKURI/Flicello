# Retrospective — 2026-10-08

Five days, 33 commits, two trips (Sydney "Give Way", Wollongong), eight rated versions. This looks back
at what actually moved the user's rating, forward at what could take a film past 6/10, and sets up the
loop that keeps improving the system.

## 1. Look back

| Date | Version | User | Critics | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10-05 | Sydney v5 | 3 | 6.88 (parent iteration) | agent loop; tram ×3, sudden cuts |
| 10-06 | Sydney v8 | 4 | — | user's fixes; outro **8** |
| 10-06 | Wollongong agent draft | "pretty bad" (~2.5) | 6.72 | car shot, voice, text, ambient music |
| 10-06 | Winter Breeze cut1 | 3 | — | template-first; shaky, dull, companion visible |
| 10-07 | cut3 | 4.2 | — | vidstab + measured shake, vivid grade; stock music |
| 10-07 | cut4 | 4.6 (music 5) | — | original ElevenLabs music |
| 10-07 | round 3 A/B/C | 4.8 | — | 3 original songs, cut from each song's bars |
| 10-08 | C polished + intro/outro | 4.8 | — | sound design, climax; intro/outro **9** |

**What moved the score:** removing negatives (shake, dull colour, forbidden content) 3 → 4.2; music
designed for the footage 4.2 → 4.6 → 4.8; pacing from the song's own bars. **What didn't:** sound
design, the new climax and frame-exact sync left the film at 4.8 — they fixed things the user only
notices when they're wrong.

**The gap that matters:** the intro/outro got 9 and the films 4.8. The 9s were *designed ideas* — a
question and its answer, a premise and a payoff. The films were *assembled footage*, however polished.
Murch's rule of six ranks emotion (51 %) and story (23 %) far above rhythm (10 %); we spent most of the
effort on rhythm and technical polish.

**Failures, honestly:**
- The critic panel scored drafts the user rated ~3 at 6.4–6.9. The loop was optimising against a judge
  3–4 points too generous — partly our own instruction ("an ordinary competent edit is a 5–6").
- Each Opus agent cost ~300K tokens of the user's Pro usage for generic results.
- Stock/free music was rejected every time; it took original composition to fix.
- Bugs that hurt the feel: a beat grid on the off-beat hi-hats, drops placed a beat early, and a frame
  rounding drift that put cuts up to 145 ms late (probably part of the Sydney "beat not syncing").
- Rules were stated but not checked mechanically (a car shot, a voice and text all reached a draft).

## 2. Look forward — research

- **Hook:** Instagram viewers decide in 1.7–2 s; a 60 %+ 3-second hold gives 5–10× reach; visual change
  every 1.5–3 s; DM shares are the strongest reach signal; 7–15 s completes best, 30–60 s earns saves.
  ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length), [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-algorithm/), [smmnut](https://smmnut.com/blog/instagram-hooks/))
- **Emotion first:** Murch's rule of six — emotion, story, rhythm, eye-trace, 2D plane, 3D space, in
  that order. ([FilmDaft](https://filmdaft.com/walter-murchs-rule-of-six-the-editors-formula-for-choosing-the-right-cut/), [StudioBinder](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/walter-murch-rule-of-six/))
- **Eye-trace:** keep the next subject where the eye already is, so fast montages read smooth.
  ([cut-daily](https://www.cut-daily.com/eye-trace-an-editors-secret-weapon/), [Artlist](https://artlist.io/blog/eye-trace-and-rule-of-six-editing/))
- **Visual beats:** align motion events inside shots with musical beats and footage appears to dance.
  ([Davis & Agrawala, SIGGRAPH 2018](https://history.siggraph.org/learning/visual-rhythm-and-beat-by-davis-and-agrawala/), [Stanford](https://engineering.stanford.edu/news/video-editing-algorithm-gives-ordinary-motion-dance-rhythm), [beat-aligned editing 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.18881))
- **Transitions:** ~90 % simple cuts; real whip pans cut mid-blur; match cuts on shape/colour/motion;
  one signature style — "technically correct but emotionally flat" comes from using every effect.
  ([No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/editing-techniques-better-travel-videos), [Beverly Boy](https://beverlyboy.com/film-technology/whip-pans-that-dont-suck-clean-transitions/), [flashboards](https://flashboards.yaroflasher.com/learn/editing/seamless-transitions/))
- **Sound:** layer the place's ambience under the music. ([Beverly Boy](https://beverlyboy.com/film-technology/cinematic-travel-video-editing-tools-tips-and-techniques/))
- **LLM editing systems:** VLM descriptions + LLM storyboarding (LAVE), offering alternatives
  (VideoDiff), simulated audiences (SimTube). ([LAVE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10294), [VideoDiff](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.10190), [SimTube](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.09577))
- **Judging:** LLM judges agree with people in pairwise comparisons, poorly on absolute scores; calibrate
  against human labels. ([Eugene Yan](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/llm-evaluators/), [pairwise preference](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.16950))
- **Self-improvement:** Reflexion-style post-mortems written to memory and read before the next try.
  ([Reflexion](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Reflexion:-an-autonomous-agent-with-dynamic-memory-Shinn-Labash/46299fee72ca833337b3882ae1d8316f44b32b3c), [ERL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24639))
- **Shot quality:** DOVER scores aesthetic and technical quality separately (open source).
  ([DOVER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.04894))
- **Music timing:** ElevenLabs composition plans have `respect_sections_durations` for strict section
  lengths. ([ElevenLabs SDK](https://www.mintlify.com/elevenlabs/elevenlabs-python/api-reference/audio/music))

## 3. What changed today (the playbook — done)
- `config/lessons.md`: the user's scale with real anchors, what moved it, standing rules, process,
  craft to reach for, and dated post-mortems. Every agent reads it first.
- `config/ratings.jsonl`: every rating with the critics' prediction, to measure the bias.
- Critics, judge and adversary score on the user's scale; the judge leans on pairwise strength.
- Director: emotion → story → rhythm; premise, arc and payoff before shots; hook in 1.5–2 s.
- Editor: visual beats, eye-trace, in-camera transitions, ~90 % plain cuts, the breathing moment.
- `/edit-video`: the template-first path that worked, with two approval gates and a learning step.
- `docs/shooting-guide.md`: what to shoot so the edit can pass 6.

## 4. Roadmap (code — needs the user's go)
**P0 — make the loop measure itself**
1. `vido rate`: log a rating, the critics' prediction and a post-mortem; show the critic bias.
2. `vido check`: the QA gate run before every reveal (beat error from scene changes, black frames,
   loudness/true peak, voices in used clip sound, contact sheet for companion/car/text).
3. `vido template design`: the round-3 designer as product code (bar patterns, pools by role, gates,
   automatic sound design, intro/outro).
4. Frame-exact cut boundaries in the agent pipeline too (the drift fix only covers templates).

**P1 — craft levers from the research**
5. Visual beats: find in-shot motion peaks and land them on beats (pick `src_in`, micro speed warps).
6. Eye-trace aligner for fast montages (motion/saliency centroid → `zoom_center`).
7. In-camera transition finder (whip pans from pan speed; match-cut suggestions).
8. Vertical 9:16 with subject reframing, a 30–45 s Reel cut, the question in the first 1.5 s.

**P2**
9. DOVER aesthetic scores for candidate moments. 10. Sky treatment for overcast shots (graduated
filter; replacement only with consent). 11. A simulated-audience critic. 12. Strict section timing
for generated music.

## 5. The recursive loop
Rate → log (`ratings.jsonl`) → post-mortem (`lessons.md` §6) → agents read lessons first → next edit →
rate. Critics predict the user's number; when their error drifts, the anchors are rewritten. Options
pages (music, cuts) turn the user's choices into pairwise signal. Every ~3 rated finals, a short retro
like this one: promote the rules that mattered, delete the ones that didn't.
