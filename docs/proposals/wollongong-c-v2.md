# Proposal — Wollongong C v2: "The walk to the edge"

Same footage, same music (C Cinematic Horizon), rebuilt around what the night research found missing:
a person with a reason, a turn at the midpoint, one real scene, a silence beat and slower pacing. Not
rendered — waiting for the user's go.

**Premise (one line):** he walked to the edge of the land to see what the sea does.
**Arc:** stillness → the sea gathering power → he reaches the edge (the turn: silence) → what he sees
erupts below him → he sits, the sea breathes → sky.

| Music | Time | What happens | Pacing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Piano intro | 0–14 s | Arrival: the still tide pool, the lighthouse. He steps into the landscape (rise reveal of him on the rock). Natural sound under the piano | 4–5 s shots |
| Riser | 14–16 s | The sea stirs: the wave curling, a stutter | builds |
| Drop 1 | 16–30 s | The sea's power on the rock shelf: the explosion in slow motion, then a burst of 1-beat cuts against slow-motion holds; the far shore for breath | bursts + holds |
| Dip (the turn) | 30–32 s | He walks up to the cliff edge (slow motion, from behind), past his shoulder — **the music drops to near silence**, only wind | one long shot |
| Drop 2 | 32–46 s | **What he sees** (POV): straight down at the teal swirl hits on the beat with the music punching back in; the surf bursting against the columns, the boulders, the swell — the cliff sea from his eyes, cut on phrases | POV scene |
| Last bar of the peak | 46–48 s | His reaction: him seated at the edge, still, facing it all | hold |
| Outro | 48–54 s | The sea breathes; tilt up into the sky (button) | long hold |

**What changes from v1**
- A turn at 0:30 (silence, the walk) instead of a breather between two montages.
- One scene: walks → looks → what he sees → reaction (Kuleshov order), the climax built from it.
- ~30 shots instead of 42: about 1.8 s per shot instead of 1.3 s; story moments 3–6 s.
- Sound: J-cut the cliff wind into the walk; the swirl's roar lands with the drop-2 hit; real silence
  at the turn.
- Fewer effects: flash only on the two drops; most cuts plain.

**Cost:** no new music (C is chosen); one `vido template design` + one render (~10 min), checked by
`vido check` before it's shown.
